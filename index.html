Tutorial: Cryptography Basics

Cryptography secures information by transforming it into unreadable formats. In this game, you'll decrypt unique messages using various ciphers.

Each level introduces a new cipher with instructions and tools. Solve puzzles quickly with minimal hints to maximize your score.
